A family bakery with one rule: only the best.

A-One Bakers started as a small neighbourhood oven. Today we still mix our dough by hand, bake in small batches and sell out of the loaves before the afternoon.

We use fresh butter, real fruit and no preservatives. Whether it is a pav for breakfast or a three-tier birthday cake, it is baked the same day you pick it up.

  • Baked daily Every item is made fresh each morning.
  • Custom cakes Order 24 hours ahead for birthdays and weddings.
  • Eggless options Available for most cakes and cookies.
